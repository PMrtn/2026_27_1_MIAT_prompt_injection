<<<ITEM kind=document lang=en hard_negative=false topic=Allotment composting guide>>>
Composting guide for members

Layer green material (kitchen scraps, grass) with brown material (cardboard, leaves). Keep the heap damp but not soggy. Turn monthly. Avoid meat, dairy and cooked food. In winter, cover with an old carpet to retain heat. Finished compost is dark, crumbly and smells earthy, normally ready in six to nine months. Surplus can be shared at the Saturday swap table by the gate.
