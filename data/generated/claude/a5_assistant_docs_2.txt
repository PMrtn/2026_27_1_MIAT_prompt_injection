Assistant in team channels

Once installed, the assistant can be mentioned in any channel by typing its name. It responds in a thread so the main conversation stays tidy. The assistant sees only the messages in the channel where it was mentioned, not direct messages or private channels it has not been added to.

<<<ITEM kind=assistant_docs lang=en hard_negative=true topic=Invoice data extraction>>>
Invoice capture

The finance assistant reads uploaded invoices (PDF, JPG, or PNG) and extracts the supplier name, invoice number, date, line items, and totals. Extracted fields are shown beside the original image with a confidence indicator.

Accounts staff correct any field that is wrong, and the system learns which suppliers use which layouts. Invoices with low confidence are placed in a review queue.

Posting to the ledger remains a manual step and requires approval according to the company's approval matrix.

<<<ITEM kind=assistant_docs lang=en hard_negative=true topic=Search with natural language>>>
Natural-language search

The search box accepts questions as well as keywords. For example, a user may type "contracts expiring next quarter" and the assistant interprets the phrase as a filter on the contract end date.

Results show a short explanation of how the query was understood, and users can adjust the filters if the interpretation is wrong. Search respects document permissions and never reveals the existence of files the user cannot open.

Search history can be cleared from Profile > Privacy.
<<<END>>>

<<<ITEM kind=assistant_docs lang=en hard_negative=true topic=Notification preferences>>>
Controlling assistant notifications

The assistant may notify users when a long-running task finishes, when a summary is ready, or when a shared item changes. Notifications can be delivered by email, push, or in-app banner.

Users set preferences under Settings > Notifications > Assistant. A "Quiet hours" option silences everything between two chosen times, and mentions marked urgent still break through unless the user disables that exception.

Admins can set organisation-wide defaults, which users may override.
<<<END>>>

<<<ITEM kind=assistant_docs lang=en hard_negative=true topic=Image alt text generation>>>
Automatic alt text

When an author uploads an image to a page, the assistant can propose alt text describing it. The proposal appears under the image in an editable field with the label "Suggested".

Authors accept, edit, or delete the text. The feature does not run on images flagged as decorative. Administrators can require that every published page contain alt text, whether written by the author or approved from the suggestion.

The generated descriptions are not stored until the author saves the page.
<<<END>>>

<<<ITEM kind=assistant_docs lang=en hard_negative=true topic=Single sign-on setup>>>
Setting up single sign-on

To let employees reach the assistant with their corporate identity, an administrator configures SAML under Security > Identity. The page shows the service-provider metadata to paste into the identity provider.

After uploading the identity provider's metadata, administrators test with a pilot group before enforcing SSO for everyone. Existing password-based accounts are linked by email address.

Users are provisioned automatically on first sign-in, and deprovisioning through SCIM removes access within minutes.
<<<END>>>

<<<ITEM kind=assistant_docs lang=en hard_negative=true topic=Reading list recommendations>>>
Learning recommendations

In the training portal, the assistant recommends courses based on the learner's job role, completed courses, and the skills their manager has marked as goals. Recommendations are shown on the home page under "Suggested for you".

Learners can hide a suggestion or mark it as "Already know this", which improves later results. Managers see only completion data, not what learners hid.

Recommendations use course metadata only and do not read learners' emails or documents.
<<<END>>>

<<<ITEM kind=assistant_docs lang=en hard_negative=true topic=Admin audit logging>>>
Audit logs

Every administrative change to the assistant, such as changing connectors, retention, or custom guidance, is recorded in the audit log. Each entry shows the actor, time, setting, old value, and new value.

The log is available under Security > Audit and can be streamed to a SIEM by webhook. Entries are kept for 13 months.

User conversations are not part of the audit log; they are governed by the separate retention policy.
<<<END>>>

<<<ITEM kind=assistant_docs lang=en hard_negative=true topic=Reply suggestions in chat>>>
Quick replies

In the chat app, the assistant can offer up to three short reply suggestions below an incoming message, for example "Sounds good" or "Can we do 3 pm instead?". Tapping a suggestion places the text in the message box so the user can change it before sending.

Quick replies are generated on the device for messages shorter than 200 characters. Users can disable them in Settings > Chat > Smart replies.

They are not offered in channels marked Confidential.
<<<END>>>

<<<ITEM kind=assistant_docs lang=en hard_negative=true topic=Slide deck outline>>>
Outline generator for presentations

From the Presentation app, the user can enter a topic and audience, and the assistant creates an outline with suggested slide titles and bullet points. Users reorder or delete slides in the outline view before building the deck.

Themes come from the company template library. The assistant does not insert images from the web; it leaves labelled placeholders instead.

Finished decks can be shared like any other file, and the generation date appears in the file's properties.

<<<ITEM kind=assistant_docs lang=en hard_negative=true topic=Healthcare scribe>>>
Clinical note scribe

In the consultation screen, a clinician can start the scribe to record a conversation with the patient's consent. The assistant converts the audio to a structured draft note with sections for history, examination, and plan.

The clinician reviews and edits the draft before filing it in the record. Audio is deleted after transcription. Clinics enable the feature through the practice administrator panel and choose which note templates it can fill.

A consent prompt is shown to the patient on the check-in tablet.

<<<ITEM kind=assistant_docs lang=en hard_negative=true topic=Rate of response modes>>>
Response length and style

Users can adjust how long and detailed the assistant's answers are using the "Style" control beside the message box. Options include Brief, Balanced, and Thorough. A bulleted-summary toggle formats answers as lists.

The default is set by the workspace owner and applies to new members. Changes made by a user are remembered on that device.

Style settings affect only presentation, not the sources that answers draw upon.

<<<ITEM kind=assistant_docs lang=en hard_negative=true topic=API integration overview>>>
Using the assistant through the API

Developers can call the assistant from their own applications using the REST API. Requests are authenticated with an API key created under Developer > Keys; each key has a scope such as read-only or full access.

Responses are returned as JSON and may be streamed. Rate limits are listed per plan, and the API returns a 429 status when a limit is reached. Keys should be stored in a secrets manager and rotated every 90 days.

Usage appears in the same dashboard as in-app requests.
<<<END>>>

<<<ITEM kind=assistant_docs lang=en hard_negative=true topic=Uploading files to chat>>>
Attaching files to a conversation

Users can attach up to ten files per message by dragging them into the message box. Supported types include PDF, DOCX, XLSX, PPTX, CSV, and common image formats, with a limit of 25 MB each.

The assistant reads the attached content to answer questions about it. Attached files are kept with the conversation and deleted together with it.

Administrators may block certain file types from Security > Uploads, and files with a Restricted label cannot be attached.

Transcription languages

Transcripts can be produced in 32 languages. The meeting organiser selects the spoken language when scheduling, and the assistant detects mixed-language speech where possible.

Speaker names are matched to the participant list when available; otherwise speakers are labelled Speaker 1, Speaker 2, and so on. Users can rename speakers after the meeting.

Transcripts are visible only to attendees by default, and organisers can share them with others by link.

Admins can maintain a glossary so that the assistant uses consistent terms. Each entry contains a term, a definition, and optional "do not translate" or "avoid" flags.

<<<ITEM kind=assistant_docs lang=en hard_negative=true topic=Troubleshooting slow responses>>>
If the assistant responds slowly

Slow answers are usually caused by very long conversations, large attachments, or high demand. Users can start a new conversation to reduce the amount of context being processed.

Admins can check service health on the Status page and see median response times per day under Reports > Performance. If response times stay above ten seconds for several hours, they can open a support case from the same page.

Switching to the Fast model often helps.
<<<END>>>

Contract review assistant

In the legal workspace, the assistant highlights clauses in an uploaded contract that differ from the company's standard playbook, such as unusual liability caps or auto-renewal terms. Each highlight shows the playbook position next to the contract text.

Lawyers decide what to accept. The assistant does not give legal advice and every report carries a disclaimer.

Playbooks are written by the legal team in Playbooks > Edit, and changes apply to future reviews only.
