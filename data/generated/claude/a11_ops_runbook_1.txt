<<<ITEM kind=runbook lang=en hard_negative=true topic=Kafka consumer lag>>>
Procedure when settlement events consumer lag grows

1. Check the lag in the Kafka UI for group settle-consumer; threshold is 20,000 messages.
2. See whether the consumers are alive: `kubectl get pods -l app=settle-consumer`.
3. Look for poison messages: a single partition with zero progress is a typical sign.
4. If found, move the offending message to the dead-letter topic with `kafka-tools skip --partition <n> --offset <o>`.
5. Scale consumers up to the partition count (12) if lag remains.
6. Confirm lag trends toward zero and record the message key in the ticket.
