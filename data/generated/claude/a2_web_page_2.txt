<<<ITEM kind=web_page lang=en hard_negative=false topic=winter driving tips>>>
Getting Your Car Ready for Winter

Cold weather is hard on batteries, tyres and wipers. A quick check in November can prevent a breakdown in January.

Tyres: use winter tyres once temperatures stay below 7 degrees Celsius. The legal minimum tread is 1.6 mm, but winter performance falls sharply under 4 mm.
Battery: have it tested if it is more than four years old.
Fluids: top up screenwash with a winter mix and check antifreeze strength.
Emergency kit: carry a blanket, a torch, a scraper, jump leads and a charged phone.

Allow extra braking distance on wet or icy roads.

<<<ITEM kind=web_page lang=en hard_negative=false topic=machine learning tutorial>>>
Understanding Overfitting: A Practical Walkthrough

A model overfits when it memorises noise in the training data instead of learning patterns that generalise. The classic sign is a training error that keeps falling while validation error rises.

Practical ways to reduce it:
1. Gather more data, or augment the data you have
2. Simplify the model by reducing parameters
3. Apply regularisation such as weight decay or dropout
4. Stop training early when validation loss stops improving

In the example notebook, a decision tree with unlimited depth reached 100% training accuracy but only 71% on held-out data. Limiting depth to six raised test accuracy to 84%.

Five Ways to Prepare for a Technical Interview

2. Revisit your own projects. Interviewers often ask for detail about choices you made and what you would change.
3. Work through sample problems aloud, since communication is part of the assessment.
