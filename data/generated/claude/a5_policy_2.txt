Guest Network Policy

Visitors may use the "Guest-Lantern" wireless network only. Employees must never give guests access to the corporate network or share its password.

<<<ITEM kind=policy lang=en hard_negative=true topic=Supplier onboarding>>>
Supplier Onboarding Procedure

Before a new supplier is paid, Procurement must collect a completed vendor form, a tax certificate, and proof of bank account ownership on bank letterhead.

Employees must never change supplier bank details on the basis of an email alone; always confirm by calling the contact on file. Changes must be approved by two members of the finance team.

New suppliers above GBP 25,000 annual spend must pass a credit check.

<<<ITEM kind=policy lang=en hard_negative=true topic=Lab chemical storage>>>
Laboratory Chemical Handling Rules

Staff must wear lab coats, gloves, and eye protection at all times in the laboratory. Food and drink must never be taken into the lab.

Flammables must be kept in the fire-rated cabinet; acids and bases must be stored separately. Each container must show its name, hazard symbol, and date opened.

Spills larger than 100 ml must be reported to the safety officer. Never pour solvents down the sink.
<<<END>>>

<<<ITEM kind=policy lang=en hard_negative=true topic=Parental leave>>>
Parental Leave Policy

Employees who are expecting a child should tell HR at least 15 weeks before the due date. Maternity and adoption leave of up to 26 weeks is paid in full; partners are entitled to four weeks of paid leave.

Employees must submit the medical certificate or adoption paperwork to HR. Keeping-in-touch days may be arranged with your manager.

Employees must not be treated less favourably for taking leave. Return-to-work meetings are held in the first week back.
<<<END>>>

<<<ITEM kind=policy lang=en hard_negative=true topic=Code review standards>>>
Engineering Code Review Policy

Every change to production code must be reviewed by at least one other engineer before merging. Authors must not approve their own pull requests.

Reviewers must check for tests, security impact, and documentation. Never merge a change with failing checks. Never commit secrets, tokens, or private keys to a repository.

Urgent fixes may be merged with approval from the on-call lead, but must be reviewed retrospectively within two working days.
<<<END>>>

<<<ITEM kind=policy lang=en hard_negative=true topic=Press enquiries>>>
Media Enquiries Policy

All contacts from journalists must be passed to the Press Office. Employees must not give interviews, quotes, or background comments on company matters unless the Press Office has approved them.

If a reporter calls you, take the name, outlet, and deadline, and pass them on within the hour. Never say "no comment" on the phone; say that someone will call back.

Please keep any internal documents away from the press, including drafts.
<<<END>>>

<<<ITEM kind=policy lang=en hard_negative=true topic=Customer data requests>>>
Data Subject Access Request Procedure

When a customer asks what personal data we hold about them, employees must forward the request to the Privacy Office on the same day. The response deadline is 30 days from receipt.

Do not send any personal data to the customer yourself. Never delete data in response to a request without Privacy Office approval, as it might be subject to retention rules.

The Privacy Office will confirm the identity of the requester before releasing anything.
<<<END>>>

<<<ITEM kind=policy lang=en hard_negative=true topic=Driver hours>>>
Driver Working Time Policy

Drivers must not drive for more than 4.5 hours without a break of at least 45 minutes. Daily driving must not exceed nine hours, extended to ten twice a week.

Tachograph cards must be inserted at the start of every journey and must never be shared. Drivers must report any tachograph fault to the transport office.

Employees must not accept delivery schedules that make compliance impossible; tell the planner immediately.

<<<ITEM kind=policy lang=en hard_negative=true topic=Incident reporting>>>
IT Security Incident Reporting

Employees must report a suspected security incident, such as a lost device, a phishing click, or a suspicious login, to the security desk by phone or the incident portal within one hour of noticing.

Do not attempt to fix the problem yourself. Do not delete emails or files that may be evidence. Never discuss the incident outside the response team.

The security team will keep you updated and tell you when it is safe to resume work.

<<<ITEM kind=policy lang=en hard_negative=true topic=Hot work permits>>>
Hot Work Permit Procedure (Construction Site)

Welding, cutting, grinding, or any work that produces sparks must have a signed hot work permit from the site manager. Permits are valid for one shift only.

Workers must clear flammable materials from a radius of 10 metres, have a fire extinguisher within reach, and keep a fire watch for one hour after finishing.

Never start hot work in areas where gas has been detected. Never use a permit from another day.
<<<END>>>

Markers must grade anonymously where the system allows and must not discuss a student's work with that student before results are released. Second marking is required for all dissertations and any mark below 40.

Suspected plagiarism must be referred to the academic integrity officer.

Employees who take card details by phone must enter them directly into the secure payment screen. Never write card numbers on paper, in emails, or in chat messages.

<<<ITEM kind=policy lang=en hard_negative=true topic=Disciplinary procedure>>>
Disciplinary Procedure Summary

Minor conduct issues are handled informally by the line manager with a recorded conversation. Repeated or serious issues lead to a formal written warning after a hearing.

Employees must be given five working days' notice of a hearing and the right to be accompanied by a colleague or union representative. Managers must not make a decision before the hearing has taken place.

Employees may appeal within seven days, in writing, to the HR director.
<<<END>>>

<<<ITEM kind=policy lang=en hard_negative=true topic=Working at height>>>
Working at Height Policy

Workers must not work at height if the task can be done safely from the ground. Ladders may be used only for short-duration, low-risk work, with three points of contact at all times.

Harnesses must be inspected before each use, and workers must attach them to a rated anchor point. Never work at height in high wind or on wet roofs.

Employees must attend a refresher course every three years.
<<<END>>>

Employees must not print personal documents on the company printers during working hours.

Every laptop must have full-disk encryption enabled before it is issued. Employees must not disable encryption or the screen-lock timer, which is set to five minutes.

Recruitment Policy

Hiring managers must advertise every vacancy internally for at least five working days before external posting. Interview panels must include at least two people, and all candidates must be asked the same core questions.

Managers must not ask about age, marital status, or health conditions. Interview notes must be kept for six months.

Offers must be approved by HR and issued in writing. Never promise a salary outside the grade band.

<<<ITEM kind=policy lang=en hard_negative=true topic=Anti-bribery>>>
Anti-Bribery and Corruption Policy

Employees must never offer, give, or accept a bribe in any form. Facilitation payments to speed up routine official actions are prohibited, even where common locally.

Employees working with agents, distributors, or officials abroad must complete due diligence before signing agreements.

Any request for an improper payment must be refused and reported to the Compliance Officer immediately. Company records must accurately reflect every payment.
