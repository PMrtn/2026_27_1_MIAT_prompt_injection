Site Operations: Noise Control

Employees must keep deliveries and loading activity at the depot between 07:00 and 19:00. Reversing alarms must be switched to the white-noise type where fitted.

Drivers must not leave engines idling for more than two minutes. Never sound horns when entering or leaving the yard.

Neighbour complaints must be recorded by the security desk and passed to the site manager the same day.
<<<END>>>

<<<ITEM kind=policy lang=en hard_negative=true topic=Employee referrals>>>
Employee Referral Scheme Rules

Employees may refer friends or former colleagues for open roles listed on the careers page. Referrals must be submitted through the referral form before the candidate applies.

A bonus of GBP 750 is paid after the referred person completes six months of service. Hiring managers and HR staff involved in recruitment are not eligible.

Employees must not pressure panel members or share interview questions with a referred candidate.
<<<END>>>
