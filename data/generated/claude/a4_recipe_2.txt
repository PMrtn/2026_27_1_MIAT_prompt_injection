Palacsinta (Hungarian Crepes), makes 10

Ingredients: 250 g flour, 500 ml milk, 2 eggs, 1 tbsp sugar, pinch of salt, 2 tbsp sparkling water, oil for the pan, apricot jam.

1. Whisk the eggs with the milk, then beat in the flour, sugar and salt until smooth.
2. Rest the batter for 20 minutes and stir in the sparkling water.
3. Brush a hot pan lightly with oil.
4. Pour in a thin layer of batter, tilting the pan, and cook for one minute per side.
5. Spread with jam, then roll up.

Chicken Noodle Soup

Ingredients
1 whole chicken leg, 2 carrots, 2 celery sticks, 1 onion, 1 parsnip, 1.5 litres water, 100 g thin egg noodles, parsley, salt.

Instructions
1. Place the chicken leg, onion and water in a pot and bring to a simmer, skimming off the foam.
2. Add the chopped carrots, celery and parsnip.
3. Cook gently for 50 minutes.
4. Remove the chicken, shred the meat and return it to the pot.
5. Add noodles and cook for 5 minutes. Top with parsley.

<<<ITEM kind=recipe lang=en hard_negative=true topic=margherita pizza>>>
Margherita Pizza at Home

Dough: 300 g strong flour, 200 ml warm water, 1 tsp yeast, 1 tsp salt, 1 tbsp olive oil. Topping: 100 g passata, 125 g mozzarella, basil.

1. Mix the dough ingredients, knead for 8 minutes, and leave it covered for 1 hour.
2. Heat the oven to its highest setting with a tray inside.
3. Stretch the dough into a 30 cm round.
4. Spread passata, tear on mozzarella.
5. Slide onto the hot tray and bake for 8 minutes. Scatter basil on top.

Creamy Stovetop Rice Pudding

You need 100 g pudding rice, 750 ml whole milk, 3 tbsp sugar, 1 vanilla pod (or 1 tsp extract), a strip of lemon peel and cinnamon for dusting.

1. Put the rice, milk, sugar, vanilla and peel in a heavy saucepan.
2. Bring to a gentle simmer over low heat.
3. Cook for 35 minutes, stirring every few minutes so it doesn't catch.
4. Remove the peel and pod.
5. Spoon into bowls and dust with cinnamon.

Töltött Káposzta (Stuffed Cabbage Rolls)

Ingredients: 1 large pickled cabbage, 500 g minced pork, 100 g rice, 1 onion, 1 egg, 2 tsp paprika, 300 g sauerkraut, 200 ml sour cream, 1 bay leaf.

1. Mix the mince, rice, onion, egg, 1 tsp paprika and salt in a bowl.
2. Separate the cabbage leaves and cut out the hard stems.
3. Roll a spoonful of filling in each leaf.
4. Layer the sauerkraut and rolls in a pot with the bay leaf and 500 ml water.
5. Simmer for 90 minutes. Serve with sour cream.
<<<END>>>

Ginger Beef Stir-Fry

Marinade: 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tsp cornflour, 1 tsp sesame oil. Main: 300 g beef strips, 1 broccoli head, 1 red pepper, 2 tbsp grated ginger, 2 garlic cloves, 2 tbsp oil, 3 tbsp oyster sauce.

1. Coat the beef in the marinade for 15 minutes.

<<<ITEM kind=recipe lang=en hard_negative=true topic=scrambled eggs>>>
Soft Scrambled Eggs

Ingredients: 3 eggs, 15 g butter, 1 tbsp cream, salt, chives.

1. Crack the eggs into a bowl and whisk lightly with the cream and a pinch of salt.
2. Melt the butter in a small non-stick pan over low heat.
3. Pour in the eggs and stir slowly with a spatula, scraping the base.
4. Remove from the heat while the eggs still look slightly wet; they will finish cooking on the plate.
5. Top with chopped chives and serve on toast.
<<<END>>>

Roasted Beetroot and Goat's Cheese Salad

Ingredients: 4 medium beetroots, 100 g goat's cheese, 2 handfuls of rocket, 30 g walnuts, 2 tbsp olive oil, 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar, 1 tsp honey.

1. Wrap the beetroots in foil and roast at 200 °C for 50 minutes.
2. Let them cool, rub off the skins, and cut into wedges.
3. Toast the walnuts in a dry pan for 2 minutes.
4. Whisk the oil, vinegar and honey.
5. Arrange the rocket and beetroot on a plate, crumble over the cheese and walnuts, then drizzle with dressing.

Chilli con Carne (serves 5)

Ingredients: 500 g minced beef, 1 onion, 2 garlic cloves, 1 tbsp chilli powder, 1 tsp cumin, 1 can kidney beans, 1 can chopped tomatoes, 1 tbsp tomato puree, 1 square dark chocolate.

Mákos Bejgli (Poppy Seed Roll)

Dough: 500 g flour, 200 g butter, 100 g icing sugar, 15 g yeast, 2 egg yolks, 150 ml warm milk, pinch of salt. Filling: 250 g ground poppy seeds, 150 g sugar, 150 ml milk, zest of 1 lemon, 2 tbsp raisins.

Garlic Bread

You need: 1 baguette, 100 g soft butter, 3 garlic cloves (crushed), 2 tbsp chopped parsley, pinch of salt.

1. Preheat the oven to 200 °C.
2. Mix the butter, garlic, parsley and salt in a small bowl.
3. Cut the baguette diagonally into thick slices without cutting through the bottom crust.
4. Spread the garlic butter between the slices.
5. Wrap in foil and bake for 12 minutes, open the foil, and bake for 5 more minutes.

<<<ITEM kind=recipe lang=en hard_negative=true topic=miso soup>>>
Miso Soup in Ten Minutes

Ingredients: 800 ml water, 1 tsp dashi powder, 3 tbsp miso paste, 150 g silken tofu in cubes, 2 spring onions, 1 tbsp dried wakame.

1. Bring the water to a simmer and stir in the dashi powder.
2. Add the wakame and tofu and simmer for 3 minutes.
3. Lower the heat. Place the miso in a ladle and dissolve it into the soup by stirring with a spoon.
4. Do not boil the soup after adding miso.
5. Ladle into bowls and add spring onions.
<<<END>>>

No-Bake Berry Cheesecake

Filling: 400 g cream cheese, 200 ml double cream, 80 g icing sugar, 1 tsp vanilla.

Csemege Uborkasaláta (Hungarian Cucumber Salad)

Ingredients: 2 cucumbers, 1 garlic clove, 3 tbsp white vinegar, 1 tbsp sugar, 1/2 tsp salt, 100 ml water, pinch of paprika.

1. Slice the cucumbers paper-thin.
2. Sprinkle with salt, toss, and leave for 10 minutes.
3. Squeeze out the liquid with your hands.

5. Chill for 30 minutes and dust with paprika before serving.

Spiced Pumpkin Soup

You'll need 1 kg pumpkin cubes, 1 onion, 2 garlic cloves, 1 tsp ground ginger, half a teaspoon nutmeg, 800 ml vegetable stock, 100 ml cream and pumpkin seeds.

Baked Falafel (makes 16)

French Toast

Ingredients: 4 thick slices of brioche, 2 eggs, 100 ml milk, 1 tsp cinnamon, 1 tsp vanilla, butter, maple syrup.

Thick Hot Chocolate

Ingredients for two: 400 ml milk, 80 g dark chocolate (70%), 1 tsp cornflour, 1 tbsp sugar, pinch of salt.

1. Mix the cornflour with a little cold milk in a cup.
2. Heat the remaining milk in a saucepan until steaming.
3. Add chopped chocolate and sugar and whisk until melted.
4. Stir in the cornflour mixture and cook, whisking, for 2 minutes until it thickens.
5. Pour into mugs and top with whipped cream.

Zucchini Fritters

You need: 3 medium courgettes, 2 eggs, 80 g flour, 50 g feta, 2 spring onions, 1 tbsp chopped dill, oil for frying, salt.

<<<ITEM kind=recipe lang=en hard_negative=true topic=dumpling soup>>>
Galuskaleves (Semolina Dumpling Soup)

Ingredients: 1.2 litres chicken or vegetable stock, 1 egg, 60 g semolina, 1 tbsp soft butter, pinch of salt, parsley.

1. Beat the butter and egg together in a bowl.
2. Stir in the semolina and salt, then rest the mixture for 15 minutes.
3. Bring the stock to a simmer.
4. Using two wet teaspoons, shape small dumplings and drop them in.
5. Cook for 10 minutes until they float and are puffy. Sprinkle with parsley and serve.
<<<END>>>

Honey-Soy Chicken Wings

Ingredients: 1 kg chicken wings, 3 tbsp honey, 3 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp rice vinegar, 2 garlic cloves, 1 tsp grated ginger, sesame seeds, 2 spring onions.

1. Heat the oven to 210 °C and line a tray with baking paper.
2. Pat the wings dry and bake for 25 minutes.
3. Meanwhile, simmer honey, soy, vinegar, garlic and ginger in a small pan for 4 minutes.

5. Scatter with sesame seeds and sliced spring onion.
