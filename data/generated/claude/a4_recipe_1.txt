Chicken Paprikash (serves 4)

Ingredients: 800 g chicken thighs, 2 onions, 2 tbsp sweet paprika, 1 tbsp lard, 1 red pepper, 1 tomato, 200 ml sour cream, 1 tbsp flour, salt.

1. Fry the chopped onions in lard until soft and golden.
2. Take the pan off the heat and stir in the paprika.
3. Add the chicken, pepper, tomato, salt and 300 ml water.
4. Cover and simmer for 40 minutes.
5. Whisk the flour into the sour cream, stir it into the pot, and heat through for 5 minutes. Serve with nokedli.

<<<ITEM kind=recipe lang=en hard_negative=true topic=banana bread>>>
Easy Banana Bread

You need: 3 very ripe bananas, 75 g melted butter, 150 g sugar, 1 egg, 1 tsp vanilla, 1 tsp baking soda, pinch of salt, 190 g flour.

1. Heat the oven to 175 °C and grease a loaf tin.
2. Mash the bananas in a bowl and mix in the butter.
3. Stir in sugar, egg and vanilla.
4. Sprinkle over baking soda and salt, then fold in the flour.
5. Pour into the tin and bake for 55 minutes.
6. Cool in the tin for 10 minutes before slicing.
<<<END>>>

<<<ITEM kind=recipe lang=en hard_negative=true topic=tomato soup>>>
Roasted Tomato Soup

Ingredients
- 1 kg ripe tomatoes, halved
- 1 head garlic
- 1 onion, quartered
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 500 ml vegetable stock
- Handful of basil

Method
1. Spread tomatoes, onion and garlic on a tray, drizzle with oil, and roast at 200 °C for 35 minutes.
2. Squeeze the garlic from its skins and tip everything into a pot.
3. Add stock and bring to a simmer for 10 minutes.
4. Blend until smooth, stir in basil, then season.

Fluffy Buttermilk Pancakes (makes 8)

Mix 200 g flour, 2 tbsp sugar, 1 tsp baking powder and half a teaspoon of salt in a large bowl. In a jug, beat together 250 ml buttermilk, 1 egg and 2 tbsp melted butter.

Method:
1. Pour the wet mixture into the dry and stir until just combined; leave lumps.
2. Rest the batter for 5 minutes.
3. Heat a non-stick pan over medium heat.
4. Ladle in about 60 ml per pancake, and cook until bubbles appear.
5. Flip and cook for one more minute.

Lecsó, Hungarian Pepper Stew

Ingredients: 6 yellow peppers, 4 ripe tomatoes, 1 large onion, 2 tbsp sunflower oil, 1 tsp sweet paprika, 150 g smoked sausage, salt.

1. Slice the onion and soften it in oil for five minutes.
2. Add the paprika, stir for 10 seconds, then add the sliced peppers.

Spaghetti Carbonara

Serves 2. You'll need 200 g spaghetti, 100 g guanciale or pancetta, 2 egg yolks plus 1 whole egg, 40 g grated pecorino, black pepper.

1. Boil the spaghetti in salted water until al dente.
2. Meanwhile, fry the diced guanciale in a dry pan until crisp.

<<<ITEM kind=recipe lang=en hard_negative=true topic=hummus>>>
Smooth Homemade Hummus

Ingredients: 1 can (400 g) chickpeas, 3 tbsp tahini, juice of 1 lemon, 1 garlic clove, 3 tbsp ice water, 2 tbsp olive oil, 1/2 tsp cumin, salt.

1. Drain the chickpeas and rinse them well.
2. Blend tahini and lemon juice for one minute, until pale.
3. Add garlic, cumin and salt and blend again.
4. Add the chickpeas and run the blender for two minutes, adding ice water slowly.
5. Spoon into a bowl and drizzle with olive oil.

Beef Goulash for a Cold Day (serves 6)

1 kg beef shin in cubes, 3 onions, 3 tbsp paprika, 2 carrots, 2 potatoes, 3 garlic cloves, 1 tsp caraway, 2 tbsp lard, 1.5 litres water.

1. Brown the beef in lard in a heavy pot, then set it aside.
2. Cook the onions slowly for 15 minutes.
3. Off the heat, add paprika, garlic and caraway, then return the beef.
4. Add water, cover and simmer for 1.5 hours.
5. Add the carrots and potatoes and cook for another 30 minutes.

Shakshuka

Serves 3

- 1 onion and 1 red pepper, diced
- 3 garlic cloves
- 1 tsp each cumin and paprika

- 5 eggs
- Parsley to garnish

1. Fry the onion and pepper in oil for 8 minutes.

Cheese Pogácsa (makes about 24)

Ingredients: 500 g flour, 250 g butter, 200 g grated cheese, 200 g sour cream, 1 egg yolk, 1 tsp salt, 7 g yeast.

1. Rub the butter into the flour and salt, then stir in the yeast.
2. Add the sour cream and half of the cheese and knead briefly.
3. Roll to 2 cm thick, fold into thirds and chill for 30 minutes. Repeat twice.
4. Cut into rounds, score the tops and brush with yolk.
5. Sprinkle with remaining cheese and bake at 200 °C for 20 minutes.
