<<<ITEM kind=web_page lang=en hard_negative=false topic=pension scheme guide>>>
Voluntary Pension Savings: The Basics

A voluntary pension fund lets you save for retirement on top of the state pension. Contributions of up to 20% of your annual income can qualify for a tax credit, capped at 130,000 Ft per year.

Funds differ in risk. Younger savers often choose higher-growth portfolios with more shares, moving gradually towards bonds and cash as retirement approaches.

Check the fund's annual charge, which can range from 0.5% to 1.8%. Over thirty years, a one-point difference can reduce your final pot by roughly a quarter. You can usually change providers without penalty after twelve months.

<<<ITEM kind=web_page lang=en hard_negative=false topic=cat care advice>>>
Keeping Your Indoor Cat Happy

Indoor cats live longer and safer lives, but they need stimulation. Provide a tall scratching post, window perches and at least one daily play session of fifteen minutes.

Food puzzles slow down fast eaters and engage natural hunting instincts. Rotate toys every week so they stay interesting.

Annual vet visits should include a dental check, as gum disease is common in cats over four. Fresh water must always be available; many cats prefer a fountain to a bowl. If your cat suddenly hides, stops eating or uses the litter tray differently, see your vet promptly.

<<<ITEM kind=web_page lang=en hard_negative=false topic=baking recipe>>>
Poppy Seed Beigli: A Christmas Classic

Beigli, the rolled sweet bread filled with poppy seeds or walnuts, is a staple of the Hungarian holiday table. This version makes two rolls.

Dough: 500 g flour, 250 g butter, 200 ml milk, 40 g sugar, 25 g yeast, 1 egg.
Filling: 250 g ground poppy seeds, 120 g sugar, 150 ml milk, 1 tbsp honey, lemon zest.

Prepare the dough and let it rest for an hour. Roll into rectangles, spread with filling, roll up tightly and brush with egg yolk. Bake at 180 degrees Celsius for 40 minutes. The classic marbled crust appears if you chill the rolls before baking.

<<<ITEM kind=web_page lang=en hard_negative=false topic=climate research summary>>>
Summary: Warming and Alpine Glacier Retreat

A new study from the Innsbruck Centre for Mountain Research finds that Alpine glaciers lost 5.6% of their volume between 2022 and 2025, the fastest three-year decline on record. Researchers combined satellite altimetry with field measurements from 120 glaciers.

The authors warn that glaciers below 3,000 metres may largely disappear by 2060 under current emission pathways. Meltwater peaks, they note, are likely to arrive earlier in the season, affecting hydropower and river navigation.

The paper was published in an open-access journal; the dataset is available at the centre's repository.

<<<ITEM kind=web_page lang=en hard_negative=false topic=customs import rules>>>
Importing Goods from Outside the EU: Customs Basics

Goods arriving from non-EU countries are subject to customs duty and import VAT. The amount depends on the product classification, its origin and its value.

For consignments under 150 EUR, no customs duty is due, but VAT is charged at the standard rate of 27% in Hungary. Postal and courier companies usually collect the VAT before delivery or through a small handling fee.

Commercial importers must obtain an EORI number and file customs declarations. Certain goods, such as food, plants and medicines, may require permits or inspection. Always confirm requirements before ordering.

<<<ITEM kind=web_page lang=en hard_negative=false topic=beekeeping basics>>>
Winter Preparation for Beekeepers

As temperatures fall, colonies cluster to keep the queen warm. Your job is to ensure they have enough stores and a dry hive.

Checklist for November:
- Weigh or heft hives to confirm 15 to 20 kg of honey and syrup stores
- Fit mouse guards across entrances
- Tilt hives slightly forward so condensation drains out
- Check for varroa mite counts and treat if needed before the cold sets in

Avoid opening the hive in cold weather. Listen at the side with a stethoscope or a piece of tubing instead. A gentle hum means all is well.
