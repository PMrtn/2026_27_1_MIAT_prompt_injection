KB-1184: Resetting your router

If your internet connection drops repeatedly, restart the router by unplugging the power cable for 30 seconds. After it powers on, wait about two minutes for the status light to turn solid green. A blinking amber light means the line is not detected; check the wall socket cable. If the fault continues after two restarts, a technician visit can be booked. Average visit wait time is currently 3 working days.
